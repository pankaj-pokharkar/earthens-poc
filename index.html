Hands behind every plate

Crafted by 48 women from the villages of Tumkur

What began in 2012 as a family memory of meals served on leaves is today a manufacturing unit in Karnataka. Every piece is shaped by skilled women whose steady income keeps rural families thriving, while the world uses a little less plastic.
