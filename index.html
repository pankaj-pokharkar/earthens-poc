Our journey

A village tradition, brought to the modern table.

In 2012, Ashima and Nilesh remembered the meals of their childhood: served on leaf plates, close to nature. A trip to South India showed them handcrafted palm-leaf bowls, and the idea took root. With Ashish and Deepti joining the family enterprise, Earthens opened its own unit in Tumkur in 2016.

Today 48 women from nearby villages craft every piece. Steady income for them, less single-use plastic for everyone else.
