How we check

Five checkpoints before a plate ships

Every piece passes rigorous checks to meet international food-grade and export standards. That is how we have earned the trust of buyers across Europe, the US and beyond.

  1. 1Leaf selectionOnly clean, whole, naturally fallen sheaths are used.
  2. 2WashingEach sheath is washed with water before pressing.
  3. 3Heat pressingShaped under heat and pressure, with no glue or starch.
  4. 4Piece-by-piece inspectionChecked by hand for shape, finish and strength.
  5. 5Export packingPacked in export-grade corrugated cartons.
