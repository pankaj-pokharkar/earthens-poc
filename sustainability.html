Sustainability

Made by nature, returned to nature

No tree is cut. Nothing is added.

A full circle

The life of an Earthens plate

  1. 1

    Gathered

    Sheaths fall from the palm on their own.

  2. 2

    Sun dried

    Dried slowly in the open air.

  3. 3

    Heat pressed

    Washed and pressed. No glue or starch.

  4. 4

    Your feast

    Sturdy for hot curries and sweets.

  5. 5

    Composted

    Breaks down like any fallen leaf.

  6. 6

    Regrowth

    Rich soil feeds the next palm.

After the party

How to compost palm leaf plates at home

No special bins needed. Areca leaf is plant matter, so it breaks down the same way fallen leaves do in a garden.

  1. 1

    Scrape off leftover food and break the plates into smaller pieces.

  2. 2

    Add them to your compost pit, garden bed or wet-waste bin.

  3. 3

    Keep it moist and let nature finish the job.
