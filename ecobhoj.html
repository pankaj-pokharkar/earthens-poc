Good to know

How many people does one EcoBhoj box serve?

One EcoBhoj Party Pack sets the table for 15 guests, with two plate sizes, two bowl sizes, a cup, a spoon and a napkin for each guest, plus 3 serving trays.

Can I heat food on EcoBhoj plates?

Yes. The palm leaf plates and bowls are microwave and oven safe, as printed on the box.

How do I dispose of everything after the party?

Collect the used tableware in the large compostable garbage bag included in the box, and add it to compost or wet waste.

What is the difference between the round and square EcoBhoj packs?

Only the shape of the plates and bowls. The serving trays, spoons, cups, napkins and garbage bag are the same in both.
