Our Story

A village tradition, brought to the modern table

From India to the world

Meals served on leaf plates. Cool well water. Life close to nature. Earthens began with a family remembering the villages they grew up around, and wanting the world to eat that way again.

How the seed grew

  1. 2012

    The seed is planted

    Siblings-in-law Ashima and Nilesh, inspired by India's village traditions, begin dreaming of leaf tableware for the modern table.

  2. A trip south

    Handcrafted palm-leaf bowls

    A business trip to South India reveals artisans pressing bowls from fallen Areca sheaths. The dream finds its material.

  3. The family grows

    Ashish and Deepti join

    Nilesh's brother Ashish and his wife Deepti bring fresh energy and expertise to the small family enterprise.

  4. 2016

    Our own unit in Tumkur

    A dedicated manufacturing facility opens in Tumkur, Karnataka, giving Earthens the scale and consistency to serve eco-conscious markets worldwide.

  5. Today

    From India to the world

    48 women artisans, one of the largest production capacities in India, and buyers across Europe, the US and beyond. And now, EcoBhoj for homes.

People & purpose

48 skilled women. Steady incomes. Thriving villages.

Our Tumkur unit employs 48 women from the surrounding villages. Their craftsmanship shapes every plate, and their steady income is central to why Earthens exists: helping rural families thrive while the world uses less single-use plastic.
